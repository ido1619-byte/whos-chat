Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Short version: you can play without an account, and then nothing about you is stored by us. If you sign in with Google, we store your game stats so they sync between your devices. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Playing without an account

Your score, stats, settings and the list of clips you've already seen are saved only in your own browser (its local storage). They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It uses Firebase Authentication, a Google service. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and an account ID with the game. Firebase keeps these to manage your sign-in.

In our database (Google Cloud Firestore, on servers in the United States) we store only:

  • an account ID,
  • your game stats (how many rounds you guessed after how many skips, misses, and your best streak),
  • the list of clips you've already played, so you don't get the same ones again.

Your name and picture are only shown to you, on your own screen. Other players can't see your account or your stats.

Deleting your data

Open your account (your picture at the top right of the game) and choose Delete account. This deletes your account and your synced stats. You can also email us and we'll delete them for you.

Other services the site loads

  • Twitch: clips play in Twitch's own player. When it loads, Twitch may set cookies and collect data under the Twitch Privacy Notice.
  • Google Fonts: the site's fonts are loaded from Google.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: counts visits to the site (pages viewed, country, browser and device type). It doesn't collect personal data or track individual visitors.
  • Emote images are loaded from Twitch, 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ.

Like any website, these services receive your IP address when your browser loads something from them.

About the chat in the game

Each round shows a short part of a public Twitch chat replay, about 25 seconds from the moment of the clip. The names of the people who wrote the messages are replaced with made-up names, and so are names mentioned inside messages. Streamers' names are kept, because guessing them is the game.

Chat is only kept while it's still available on Twitch. When the original broadcast is no longer on Twitch (for the streamers in the game, usually within 60 days), the clip and its chat are removed from the site automatically.

If you see a message you wrote and want it removed sooner, email us and we'll remove it.

Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly store data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
